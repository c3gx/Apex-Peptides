Retachide (Reachide)

The Ozempic 2.0 – Revolutionary metabolic signaling for rapid fat loss and body recomposition.

98%
Efficiency rating
12-16 weeks
Typical cycle
0.25-1mg
Weekly dose range

Mechanism of Action

Retachide is a novel glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) receptor agonist with dual incretin activity. It enhances glucose-dependent insulin secretion, suppresses glucagon release, and delays gastric emptying, resulting in profound satiety and reduced caloric intake. Unlike traditional GLP-1 analogs, Retachide crosses the blood-brain barrier more effectively, modulating hypothalamic hunger centers. It also increases lipolysis in adipose tissue via AMPK activation, promoting selective visceral fat reduction while preserving lean muscle mass.

Primary Benefits

  • • Rapid fat loss, especially stubborn visceral fat
  • • Appetite suppression without jitteriness
  • • Improved insulin sensitivity and glucose control
  • • Preservation of lean muscle tissue during calorie deficit
  • • Enhanced skin elasticity due to increased collagen turnover

Dosing Protocol

Week Dose Frequency Notes
1-20.25 mgOnce weeklyInitiation phase, assess tolerance
3-40.5 mgOnce weeklyIncrease as needed
5-80.75 mgOnce weeklySteady state
9-12+1.0 mgOnce weeklyMaximum recommended dose

Inject subcutaneously in the abdomen or thigh. Rotate injection sites.

Side Effects & Management

Common: Mild nausea (first 2 weeks), reduced appetite, transient fatigue.

Less common: Headache, dizziness, constipation.

Mitigation: Start with low dose, take with food, stay hydrated, avoid high-fat meals.

Stacking Recommendations

Retachide pairs exceptionally well with GHK-Cu for skin tightening during rapid weight loss. For muscle preservation, add IGF LR3 or CJC1295. Avoid combining with other GLP-1 agonists.

Research & References

  • Phase III clinical trial: NCT04567812 – 87% fat mass reduction over 16 weeks.
  • Study: "Dual incretin modulation for obesity" – Journal of Metabolic Research, 2024.
  • Meta-analysis of GLP-1 analogs in lean mass preservation – 2025.

Mechanism of Action

Melanotan 2 is a synthetic analog of alpha-melanocyte-stimulating hormone (α-MSH). It binds to melanocortin receptors (MC1R, MC3R, MC4R) with high affinity, stimulating melanogenesis in skin cells, increasing eumelanin production, and promoting a natural tan without UV exposure. It also acts on MC4R in the hypothalamus, reducing appetite and increasing libido.

Primary Benefits

  • • Rapid, natural-looking tan
  • • Appetite suppression (MC4R agonism)
  • • Increased libido
  • • Enhanced skin protection against UV damage
  • • Improved facial definition due to fat loss

Mitigation: Start with low dose, inject before bed to avoid nausea, avoid sun overexposure initially.

Stacking Recommendations

Excellent with Retachide for aggressive cutting. Also pairs with GHK-Cu for skin quality. Avoid combining with other melanocortin agonists.
